Highest total after the Super Bowl wins.

Every team carries a leveling factor — a handicap set from last season's record. Each win pays that team's leveling factor, so a win by a bad team is worth more than a win by a good one. The whole game is finding teams the handicap underrates.

Two things worth knowing

Your own teams will play each other

Almost every four-team entry has games where two of its own teams meet. One of them has to win, so those points are already yours before kickoff — that is the hatched part of the bar on the standings page, and it is why nobody's floor is simply what they have banked.

A team's maximum

The most any single team can be worth is 20 × LF + 7.5: seventeen regular-season wins, a division title, and three playoff wins. Winning the division rather than a wild-card spot is worth exactly 0.5 more over a full playoff run.
